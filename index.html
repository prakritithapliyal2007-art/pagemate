How it works

Two sides of the same desk.

One place for people who need pages and people who write them — matched by subject, price and how far away they are.

Why nearby matters

The last kilometre is the whole point.

A typed PDF can travel anywhere. A handwritten record, a printed report or a lab file can't — so PageMate ranks people by how easily you can actually collect the pages.

Matched from your area

Pick a locality or share a rough location. Writers inside your radius appear first, with straight-line distance shown upfront.

Public pickup points

Metro gates, campus libraries and stationery shops instead of home addresses. Both sides see the same meeting spot.

Same-day handover

Most listings inside 3 km can be handed over the same evening, which is what saves you when the deadline is tomorrow.

Written work, honest limits

PageMate is built for drafts, notes, formatting, proofreading and reference material. Whatever you order, the submitted work must still follow your college's academic-integrity policy — that part is yours.

  • Verified samples before you pay
  • No full advance payment
  • Report & block from any listing
